Structural Hierarchy in Digital Pedagogies

In the landscape of modern education, the interface acts as the primary mediator between the student and the knowledge. The "Cognitive Ease" framework suggests that when structural elements—such as navigation, headers, and spacing—are standardized, the brain can divert metabolic resources away from decoding the UI and toward deep conceptual processing.

"The primary goal of educational design is not to impress with complexity, but to empower with clarity."
